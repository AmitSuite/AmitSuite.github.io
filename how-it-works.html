How it works

One tender, start to finish

The suite does not invent a new process. It follows the sequence the work already has, and makes sure each stage hands the next one a record that is already complete.

The sequence

Eight stages, in the order the work happens

Nothing below has to be done in isolation, and nothing has to be entered twice. Each stage writes to the same tender record the next one reads.

Tender Search

Search the portal and review what comes back

Open the CPPP scraper, choose a saved search profile and start a run. The suite attaches to your own Chrome or Edge browser, opens the portal's advanced search and fills the criteria. You solve the CAPTCHA and press Search; the scraper then reads the result pages and opens each tender's detail page to collect its fields.

Before anything is imported, every candidate is compared against what you already hold and marked New, Duplicate or Changed. You choose the rows to keep.

The automation never fills a CAPTCHA and never presses Search behind one. That step is always yours.
Tender Management

Import, categorise and organise

Tenders arrive either from a scraper run or from an Excel workbook, where you map the sheet's column headers onto tender fields once and the mapping is remembered for next time. Categories are assigned by the keyword rules you maintain, and rows are highlighted by your own conditions — approaching dates, missing values, whatever matters to you.

A tender you have manually categorised keeps that decision. Regenerating categories never overwrites a human choice.

Document Downloader

Collect the tender documents

For a shortlisted tender, the downloader opens its portal page through your browser and fetches the tender ZIP and PDF. It checks first whether the documents are already held or the tender has closed, then files what it downloads into that tender's folder and records the path on the record — so the paperwork can be found from the tender rather than from memory.

Document Generation

Generate your own paperwork

Choose the tenders and the Word templates, and the engine resolves every placeholder from the tender, your company profile, the company's bank details and settings. A validation pass runs first, marking each tag Resolved, Missing Value or Not In Registry, so a document with a gap in it is caught before it is written to disk.

Files land in the folder and under the name your configured patterns produce.

EMD Tracking

Pay the EMD, with the automation alongside you

Start an EMD run and the suite attaches to your browser and watches. It recognises which page you have reached, fills the part it is responsible for, reads back what the portal shows, and then goes quiet again. You can pause, resume or stop it at any point.

When payment succeeds, the tender is promoted from the Import stage to Master by a single shared rule — so a hand-entered payment and an automated one leave the record in exactly the same state.

One-time passwords and digital signatures are never handled by the automation, and credentials never appear in a log, a screenshot or an error message.
Document Parser

Read the receipt and link it to the tender

Payment receipts are read by the parser. Where a receipt's own text layer is unusable, the page is rendered and read optically instead, by two engines whose results are cross-checked — and any disagreement on an important field is flagged for you rather than resolved quietly.

Because a success receipt often prints neither the NIT number nor an application ID, the suite ranks candidate tenders on the evidence it does have and presents them rated best, average or poor, with the reasoning shown. Nothing is linked until you press Link.

A receipt can fill a field that is empty. It can never blank out a value the record already holds.
Financial Operations

Reconcile against the bank

Import the bank statements — PDF or Excel, several banks, several accounts, including consolidated files that change account part-way through. Overlapping downloads are expected, so duplicates are removed transaction by transaction rather than file by file.

Debits are then read as EMD payments and credits as refunds. Matching leads on identifiers — NIT number, tender ID, application ID, transaction reference — and uses amounts only as corroboration, because a round EMD figure matches every same-value transfer in a working account. A refund dated before its payment is rejected outright.

Where a bank debit includes gateway charges and taxes, the breakdown is recorded on the transaction. A tender's own EMD amount is never overwritten by a bank total.
Dashboard & Calendar

Stay ahead of the dates

The dashboard carries the running picture: total, today's, upcoming and overdue tenders, EMD committed, refund pending, refunded and held, and the same figures broken down by zone and by stage.

If you connect Google Calendar, the tenders you opt in have their dates written into a calendar you choose, with the titles, descriptions and reminders your configured patterns produce. The sync is one-way — your records are the source of truth, and the calendar only mirrors them.

Design principles

Four rules the automation always follows

These are constraints on what the suite is permitted to do, not features that can be switched off.

The operator owns the browser

It opens only on an explicit start, and the automation never closes it. Moving between screens in the application never touches it.

Human checkpoints stay human

CAPTCHA, one-time passwords, digital signatures and the final submission are never automated, in any module.

Suggest, then wait

Receipt links and bank matches are ranked proposals with their reasoning attached. A record changes when a person confirms it.

Never guess quietly

Genuine ambiguity is reported, not resolved. Duplicates are not imported, and near-duplicates are imported flagged.
