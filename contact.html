Reporting a problem

What to include

  • Which module or screen you were on
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • The application version, shown under About in the application
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes

Please do not send passwords or credentials. They are never needed to investigate a problem. If a document is needed, remove or mask account numbers first.

Privacy and legal

Data enquiries

Amit Automation Suite stores your records on your own machine, so most requests — seeing your data, correcting it, exporting it or deleting it — are things you can do directly inside the application, without asking anyone.

For anything that does need us — a question about the Privacy Policy, the Google Calendar integration, or the Terms — use the form and pick Privacy or data request.
