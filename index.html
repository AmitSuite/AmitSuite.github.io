One platform

Six areas of the tender lifecycle, in a single application

Every module shares one database, one set of master lists and one interface. Data entered once in Import is the same data the document engine, the EMD screens and the reconciliation view all read.

Tender Search

Run advanced searches on the Central Public Procurement Portal from saved search profiles, read the result pages and detail pages, and review every row before anything enters your database.

  • Reusable CPPP search profiles
  • Automatic New / Duplicate / Changed classification
  • Universal search and saved search cards inside the app

Tender Management

Import tenders from Excel with column mapping, then work them through the Import and Master stages in an Excel-like grid that remembers exactly how you left it.

  • Show, hide, reorder, resize and freeze columns — layout persists
  • Category, row-highlight and days-left rules you configure
  • Administrator-created custom fields on every tender

EMD Tracking

Follow earnest money from commitment through payment to refund, with committed, pending, refunded and held balances summarised on the dashboard and broken down by zone and by stage.

  • Assisted EMD payment on the portal, with the operator in control
  • Payment receipts read automatically and linked on your confirmation
  • Refunds detected from bank statements, never applied silently

Document Generation

Produce tender documents from your own Word templates. Placeholders resolve from the tender, your company profile, bank details and settings — and every value is validated before a file is written.

  • Batch generation across many tenders and templates at once
  • Pre-flight check marks each tag Resolved, Missing or Unregistered
  • Output folders and file names follow patterns you define
In development

Tender Submission Helper

A designed module that will assist the bid-submission run: preparing the portal session and uploading technical-cover documents, then staying in monitor mode until the acknowledgement is downloaded and recorded.

  • CAPTCHA, DSC, Freeze and final Submit stay operator-only
  • Acknowledgement filed against the right tender automatically
  • Architecture approved; not yet released

Financial Operations

Import bank statements from several banks and accounts, remove overlapping duplicates at transaction level, and match debits and credits to the tenders they belong to on identifier-first evidence.

  • Password-protected statements supported, passwords never stored in plain text
  • Gateway charges reported separately, never merged into an EMD amount
  • Excel export with summary, transaction, reconciliation and exception sheets

The workflow

From portal listing to reconciled refund

The suite follows the sequence the work already has. Each stage hands the next one a record that is already complete, so nothing has to be retyped.

Stage 01

Discover

Search the portal from a saved profile and review the results before importing.

Stage 02

Organise

Import, categorise and work the tender through the Import and Master stages.

Stage 03

Prepare

Download tender documents, generate your paperwork and pay the EMD.

Stage 04

Reconcile

Read receipts and bank statements, then confirm the matches they propose.

Security and control

Your data stays on your machine

Amit Automation Suite has no product server and no online account. It runs entirely on the operator's own Windows PC, and the automation is built to assist a person rather than replace them.

Local-first storage

Tenders, documents, settings and the audit log live in a single SQLite database in your own Windows profile. Nothing is uploaded to the developer.

Secrets never in plain text

Portal credentials, bank statement passwords and the Google sign-in token are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, and are kept out of logs, screenshots and error messages.

The operator stays in control

The automation never fills or bypasses a CAPTCHA and never handles an OTP or a digital signature. The browser is yours — it opens only when you press Start and is never closed for you.

Nothing is applied silently

Receipt links and bank matches are proposals with a confidence rating. A record changes only when a person confirms it, and duplicate transactions are never imported.

An audit trail you can read

Applied changes are written to an audit log — including the fields an operator was shown and chose to skip, not only the ones they accepted.

No tracking, no advertising

The application collects no analytics or usage telemetry, and this website runs no trackers. Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
